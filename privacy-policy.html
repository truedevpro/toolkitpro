Toolkit Pro Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Toolkit Pro ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Toolkit Pro mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information and device permissions may be handled when you use our App.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Toolkit Pro is designed primarily as a utility application and aims to minimize the collection of personal information.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide certain features, Toolkit Pro may request access to specific device capabilities.

Camera Access

Camera access is used for the QR and barcode scanner feature. The camera is used to scan codes in real time. Toolkit Pro does not intentionally store or transmit your camera feed to our servers.

Storage and Clipboard

Certain features may temporarily use device storage or the clipboard when you choose to copy scanned information, save information, or share generated content.

Flashlight / Torch

The App may use your device's camera flash hardware to provide flashlight and SOS-related functionality.

Other Device Features

Some utility features may require access to relevant Android device capabilities. Permissions are requested only when needed for the associated feature.

Toolkit Pro does not require you to create an account or log in to use its core utility features.

2. Analytics and Third-Party Services

Toolkit Pro may use third-party services such as Google Play Services, Firebase, or Google AdMob to provide app functionality, display advertisements, monitor app stability, or understand general app usage.

These third-party providers may automatically process certain information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interaction information, diagnostic information, crash information, or other technical data according to their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising

Toolkit Pro may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may use device or advertising identifiers and related information to provide, measure, and improve advertisements, subject to applicable laws and their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. Since Toolkit Pro is designed to minimize the collection of personal information, we do not maintain a user-account database for the core utility features.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Toolkit Pro is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

6. Your Choices

You can manage or revoke Android permissions through your device settings. You may also control certain advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google account settings.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Account Deletion and Data Requests

Toolkit Pro does not require a user account for its core functionality. If you have questions about data handled by the App or associated third-party services, you may contact us using the contact information below.
